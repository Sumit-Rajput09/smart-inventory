Decision moment: start of day t: stock on hand = inventory_level(t); history up to day t-1
Purging: rows whose label window reaches the next period were removed (2 day(s)) (prevents labels of one period using sales of the next).
Test period 2023-10-02..2023-12-30: 76.5% of rows are simulated stock-outs.

Operating thresholds: ML models and the rule probability use the highest threshold that still catches >= 95% of stock-outs on VALIDATION data; the rule-based trigger uses its documented threshold (probability >= 1 - service level, i.e. 'at or below the reorder point').

CAUTIONS
- The label is hypothetical (no deliveries assumed, real lost sales never observed) and the data is synthetic. In this file sales are a random share of the day's stock, so the learned model may exploit a data-generation pattern that real retail demand would not show.
- Do not present these scores as real historical stock-out performance. The rule-based risk assessment is a formula, not this classifier.
