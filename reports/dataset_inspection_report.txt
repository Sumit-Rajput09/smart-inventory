==============================================================================
FINDINGS THAT AFFECT THE PROJECT
==============================================================================
  1. category/region are NOT stable per product/store (they vary row by row) - they are attributes of the row, not master data. Category/region analysis is possible but a 'product' has no single category.
  2. the Seasonality column does not follow the calendar -> not used as a feature; use month/day-of-year instead.
  3. Demand Forecast is almost identical to Units Sold (corr 0.997). It cannot be used as an input (target leakage). It is only useful as a 'vendor forecast' benchmark, and even then it was probably generated from the actual sales.
  4. no autocorrelation: past sales carry almost no information about future sales. Expect lag/rolling-feature models to perform about the same as a simple mean baseline. This must be reported honestly, not hidden.
  5. weekday and monthly seasonality are weak (<5% / <10% spread).
  6. The dataset is SYNTHETIC (Kaggle). Results must not be presented as real business savings.
  7. There are no real stock-out labels and no supplier lead times; the rule-based stock-out analysis must use a configurable lead time, and any classifier would need clearly labelled SIMULATED labels.
